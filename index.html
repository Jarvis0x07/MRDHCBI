AUTOMATED EXPERIMENT • PAPER COMPARISON

Comparative Analysis

One-click evaluation. Clicking Analysis automatically evaluates the same six paper categories — Cartoon, CAD, Texture, Mask, Pattern and Document — with the paper's three comparison methods, the base MRDHCBI method, and our Hamming-code method. No pairing key, sender/receiver connection, or image upload is required. Literature values for Ren et al. [12], Li et al. [13], Zhang et al. [14] and the base paper are taken from the paper. Our Hamming pipeline is executed in-browser on deterministic binary replicas of those six categories because the exact BMP files named by the paper are not present in the supplied project.
