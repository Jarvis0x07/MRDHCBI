AUTOMATED EXPERIMENT • PAPER COMPARISON

Comparative Analysis

One-click evaluation. Clicking Analysis automatically evaluates the same six paper categories — Cartoon, CAD, Texture, Mask, Pattern and Document — with the paper's three comparison methods, the base MRDHCBI method, and our Hamming-code method. No pairing key, sender/receiver connection, or image upload is required. All five methods are executed locally. Ren [12], Li [13], and Zhang [14] are reference implementations of the published mechanisms; Base MRDHCBI and our Hamming method use the project's executable implementations. No published numerical result is copied into the measured tables. The exact BMP files named by the paper are not present, so the six category inputs are deterministic binary replicas.
